Principal Investigator

Dr. Igor Korover

korover@tauex.tau.ac.il

Senior Lecturer · LinkedIn · University profile · INSPIRE

Igor joined the School of Physics and Astronomy at Tel Aviv University in 2024. He was previously a senior postdoctoral fellow at MIT (2021–2024), a joint postdoctoral fellow at the Center for Frontiers in Nuclear Science at Stony Brook (2022–2024), and a researcher at the Nuclear Research Center Negev. He holds a PhD from Tel Aviv University (2015), an MSc from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev (2009) and a BA from the Technion (2004), and was a Katzir Scholar from 2015 to 2021.
