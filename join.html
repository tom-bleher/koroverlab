Fully funded MSc & PhD positions in high-energy nuclear physics

We are seeking highly motivated and talented candidates to join the High Energy Nuclear Physics Research Group at Tel Aviv University. Fully funded MSc and PhD positions are available.

Our group studies the fundamental properties of nuclear matter, including the forces between nucleons and their modifications inside nuclei. We push the limits of our understanding using high-energy, high-luminosity electron beams (GeV scale). At these energies we push beyond effective nucleon–nucleon interactions and enter the domain of QCD.

We are also part of an international collaboration to build a novel Electron–Ion Collider - a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that will help reveal key properties of the visible universe, such as the origin of mass. Our group is actively involved in designing and constructing detectors that will be integrated into this unique machine.

Research Topics

  • Nucleon structure and its modifications in the nuclear medium.
  • Nucleon–nucleon interactions.
  • Detector development for the next-generation Electron–Ion Collider.

What You'll Do

  • Participate in international experiments at leading accelerator facilities (primarily in the USA).
  • Analyze large-scale experimental data.
  • Contribute to the design and preparation of upcoming experiments.
  • Develop and test advanced particle detectors, associated electronics, and data acquisition.

What We Seek

  • High motivation – this is essential.
  • Strong interest in experimental nuclear or particle physics.
  • Enthusiasm for hands-on work with detectors, instrumentation and electronics.
  • Willingness to work in international collaborations and travel for experiments.
